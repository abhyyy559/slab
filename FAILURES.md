- Open-domain transfer: not supported. Reason: command store is site-specific.
- Live sites: the `probe` action is read-only (no login, no CAPTCHA solving, no retries on blocks). Login walls, CAPTCHAs, 403s and 429s ABSTAIN with named reasons (`auth_wall/captcha/bot_block/rate_limit`) — never bypassed. Retailers that prohibit automation or fingerprint bots (Amazon/Flipkart/Facebook-style targets) are out of scope: point the probe there and it will refuse at the wall. Practice sandboxes (books/quotes.toscrape.com) pass.
- Free-text goals now DO drive the plan, but only within one workflow, and the parser is keyword-based, not an LLM. `planner.plan_goal()` extracts budget / RAM / PIN from the goal and routes to `phone_delivery_check`; `run_variant` logs a `PLAN` event. Precedence is explicit call args > goal-derived params > command defaults. Goals whose domain has no mocks (medicine, travel, groceries) or that match no known workflow return `status: ABSTAIN, failed_constraint: unsupported_goal` instead of silently running the phone workflow. Limits to say on stage: 1 workflow, English keyword matching, and the 3-day delivery window is fixed by the workflow rather than parsed from the goal. Open-domain transfer remains unsupported.

- The console is a dark ops dashboard by design while the mock storefronts are light — they are different surfaces (operator tool vs. simulated product), so the contrast is intentional rather than an inconsistency.
